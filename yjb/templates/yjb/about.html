About YJB

Find jobs. Learn more. Get hired. YJB connects young people with job opportunities while helping employers find the right people.

Jobs meet people.

YJB is being built to make finding a job easier for young people and finding great employees easier for employers.

For Job Seekers

We believe young people deserve better access to jobs, better information about the opportunities available to them, and opportunities that match their skills and interests.

YJB helps young job seekers discover opportunities in their areas of interest and learn more about the jobs they are applying for.

For Employers

We want to make hiring easier for employers by helping them connect with young people who are actively looking for work.

YJB helps employers find young people who are interested in building their skills, gaining experience, and becoming part of a team.

STARTING IN OUR COMMUNITY

Built for North Battleford.

YJB is being developed with North Battleford and the surrounding Battlefords community in mind.

North Battleford

North Battleford is a regional hub in northwest Saskatchewan. The community serves people from surrounding communities and is connected to the region through major highways, rail, and an airport.

The City describes North Battleford as a place with employment opportunities ranging from entry-level positions to management and career-development opportunities.

That makes North Battleford a great place to start building a platform focused on helping young people discover local work, gain experience, and learn about different career paths.

LOCAL OPPORTUNITIES

There are many paths to explore.

North Battleford has a diverse local economy. The City identifies healthcare, sales, trades, transportation, and equipment operations among major employment areas.

The community also has opportunities connected to retail, food and accommodations, education, construction, and transportation.

YJB can help young people discover these opportunities and understand what different jobs can offer them.

MORE THAN A JOB BOARD

Better information means better decisions.

One of the main purposes of YJB is to give young job seekers more information about the jobs they are interested in.

Instead of simply showing a job title and an application button, we want to help people understand what a job is really like and whether it is right for them.

Learn more about the job

  • What you actually do in the job
  • Skills you can learn and develop
  • Typical hours and work schedules
  • Experience and qualifications required
  • Pay and other important job details
  • Whether the job is suitable for a first job
  • Where the experience could take you next
MORE THAN WORK

Explore

Our Goal

Help young people find jobs they want, and help employers find people who want to work.

We want YJB to start local, build strong community connections, and eventually help young people find opportunities wherever their future takes them.
